## Description (max 4000)

```
Tap the circle. Say what you need to remember, and when. TapMemo writes the title, works
out the date, and puts the event in your calendar before you've put the phone down.

"Remind me to buy milk tomorrow at 3" becomes Buy milk, tomorrow at 3:00 PM, in Calendar.
No typing, no menus, no forms.

IT UNDERSTANDS HOW PEOPLE ACTUALLY TALK
Tomorrow, the day after tomorrow, Saturday the 25th, tonight, at noon, at 9:15. And the
loose ones too: "at 3" means 3 PM, because that's what you meant — say "at 3 am" and it
takes you literally.

SEE WHAT IT HEARS
The words appear as you speak them and the waveform follows your voice, so you know it's
listening and what it caught. When the event is created, TapMemo tells you exactly what it
made and when — and one tap undoes it, or changes the time.

CALENDAR, REMINDERS, OR NEITHER
A memo with a date becomes a calendar event by itself. Swipe a memo to send it to Reminders,
to share it, or to delete it. No date? It stays a memo until you decide. Delete the event in
Apple's Calendar and TapMemo notices, and says so.

ALWAYS ONE TAP AWAY
Home Screen widgets in three sizes, Lock Screen widgets, and a Control Center button, so you
can start talking without hunting for the app.

ITALIAN AND ENGLISH
Speech recognition, date parsing and the interface all follow the language your iPhone is
set to. Both are first-class: the Italian "alle 3" and the English "at 3" get the same
careful treatment.

IT STAYS ON YOUR IPHONE
No account. No sign-up. No server. No analytics, no trackers, no third-party SDKs. Speech is
transcribed by Apple's own framework, memos are stored on your device, and the only things
TapMemo writes anywhere are the events and reminders you asked for, in your own calendar.

Free, with no purchases and nothing to subscribe to.
```
